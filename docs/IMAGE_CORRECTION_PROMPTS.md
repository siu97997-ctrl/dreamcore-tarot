## 全局风格约束

Full vertical tarot card in a nocturnal dreamcore healing style: deep indigo-black background, luminous soft gradients, thin ornate gold frame, delicate lilac flowers, and a gentle mystical atmosphere. Keep a clean blank top medallion and a clean blank bottom title panel for later professional typesetting. No letters, words, numbers, numerals, watermark, signature, or logo.

## 大阿卡纳

对 00–21 每张牌分别保留 Rider–Waite 核心象征和牌义构图，并套用全局风格约束。每张使用原大阿卡纳图作为内容参考，使用 `public/cards/cups/09_nine_of_cups.png` 作为统一视觉风格参考。

## 数量异常牌

- Six of Cups：two children exchange flowers; exactly six large identical golden cups in an obvious 2-by-3 grid; no other vessels or cup-like shapes.
- Eight of Cups：a solitary figure walks away on a moonlit path; exactly eight clearly separated golden cups; no other vessels or cup-like shapes.
- Eight of Wands：exactly eight clearly separated flying wooden wands crossing the sky; no extra sticks, branches, partial or reflected wands.
- Eight of Swords：a loosely bound blindfolded figure surrounded by exactly eight clearly separated swords; no extra, partial or reflected blades.

## 宫廷牌异常

- Knight of Wands：adult armored knight riding a dynamic horse and carrying one flowering wand; not a child, page or jack.
- Knight of Swords：adult armored knight charging forward on horseback and raising one sword; not a child, page or jack.
- Knight of Pentacles：adult armored knight seated steadily on a calm dark horse and holding one luminous pentacle; not a child, page or jack.
